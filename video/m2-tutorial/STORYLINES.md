# Storylines (words only; B was built)

Spine: one viewer, one real build (BMW M2 CS, Black Sapphire, FF-3 Pure Bronze, 21") walked through the live app. Every function is
shown on the real screen and its purpose said once.

## A. "The build sheet" (type carries the argument)
Device: the price tag grows line by line. Form: cut on the beat, giant kinetic type over cropped UI.
First two seconds: "R80 000" lands over the M2. Look: poster type, UI as texture. Sound: bed + whooshes on cuts.
Weakness: the UI shrinks to texture, so a viewer cannot learn where to click. Not built.

## B. "One build, start to finish" (RECOMMENDED, BUILT)
Device: the worked example. Form: object-to-object chain, every cut is caused by the click that ends the shot before it.
First two seconds: the finished black M2 on bronze wheels, then "What would yours look like?"; the film then rebuilds it
from the welcome screen, so the viewer ends where they began, knowing how.
Beats: hook -> welcome -> overview -> paint -> wheels -> size -> finish -> camera -> summary -> share/enquire/PDF -> wheel page + more options -> close.
Look: the product's own screens at full frame (dark stage + light panel), one cursor, one gold ring, solid callout card. Sound: natural British female voice, quiet music bed, click sounds on real clicks only.
Why it wins: a stranger can follow it and then do it.

## C. "Before and after" (life before and after)
Device: a customer guessing from catalogue photos, then the configurator. Form: split screen to one.
First two seconds: a flat wheel photo beside the black M2. Look: split panes. Sound: silence, then music on the reveal.
Weakness: the "old way" is invented footage the source does not have. Not built.
