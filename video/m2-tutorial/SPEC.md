GOAL:    A first-time visitor can configure their own BMW M on forged wheels after watching once, and knows what every control is for.
OUTPUT:  HyperFrames, 1920x1080, 30 fps, mp4. Narrated (natural British female, HeyGen) + quiet music bed + click sounds on real clicks.
TIME:    ~135 s x 30 = ~4050 frames. Scene length = its voice-over length + 0.7 s tail (generated by build.mjs from assets/voice/*.json).
LOOK:    Medium = the product's own screens, real captures of the live app at 1920x1080 (assets/shots/), never redrawn.
         Ground: the app (stage #000 with a pale floor pool; panel #1b1b1b). Ink on cards #ffffff on #0b0b0c.
         Accent: Forged Gold #CF9F52 only for the pointer ring, the function name and the end-card rule. Electric Orange #FF5500 only on the
         end card's call to action and on the real ENTER button (it is the product's). Display Satoshi 900 58 px, -1.5% tracking;
         function name Satoshi 700 28 px, +16% tracking, caps; body Satoshi 500 34 px.
         Cards: solid #0b0b0c, 10 px gold left rule, 0 24px 60px rgba(0,0,0,.45) shadow, radius 6 px, width 700 px. No glass, no glow.
OBJECTS: the cursor (white arrow, black outline, 38 px, 1.5 px shadow), the click ring (gold, 4 px stroke, 120 -> 36 px, 0.35 s),
         the target ring (gold, 4 px, 12 px outside the control's box, radius 14 px). Nothing else is drawn.
MOTION:  Camera = slow push 1.00 -> 1.07 on the shot's focus point over the whole scene, ease "sine.inOut". Cursor moves 0.7 s "power3.inOut"
         along a 40 px arc; click at its end; the screen changes on the next scene's first frame (hard cut, caused by the click).
         Card enters 10 frames "power4.out" from 24 px left, leaves 6 frames "power2.in". Stagger: ring +4 frames, card +8.
SHOTS:   see SCENES in build.mjs: one hero move each (cursor to the control). Something new every 2-4 s.
BANNED:  dark void with small floating things; glow/bloom/particles; glass cards; gradients; centred stacks; everything fading up;
         text that drifts after landing; corner HUD; three-card rows; a docked logo; title over a dimmed screenshot; stock icons;
         invented UI (no screen is drawn, only captured); any number not on the real screen; "unlock/seamless/effortless/in seconds".
QA:      frame at each click (ring on the real control); card readable at 360 px; end card holds 2.5 s; no text in the 5% safe margin.
